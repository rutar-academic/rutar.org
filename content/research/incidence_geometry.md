I am interested in the problem of studying *incidence lower bounds*.
The general setup is as follows: given a collection of geometric objects (such as points and tubes in the plane), what conditions guarantee that there is a non-trivial incidence?
This has applications to problems in fractal geometry, such as questions related to Nikodým sets and to the visibility conjecture.
